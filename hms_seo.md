# SEO Guidelines for Hutaib Machinery Product & Landing Pages

Creating SEO-optimized content for industrial machinery requires understanding both search engine requirements and buyer behavior in the B2B South Indian market. This document provides comprehensive rules, templates, and checklists for generating product pages and landing pages for hutaibmachinery.com, an industrial equipment supplier established in 1999 in Bengaluru, serving construction, manufacturing, and engineering industries across Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, South Maharashtra, and Goa.

---

## Business context and product portfolio

Hutaib Machinery and Spares operates as a wholesaler and trader of construction and industrial equipment. The primary product categories include **rebar/steel processing equipment** (bar bending machines, cutting machines, CNC steel bar benders, scrap straightening machines, ring making machines), **concrete equipment** (mixers, vibrators, cutting machines, ride-on power trowels, floaters), **compaction and road construction machinery** (road rollers, plate compactors, tamping rammers), and **material handling equipment** (building material hoists, threading machines). Extended offerings include electric motors, air compressors, power tools, woodworking machinery, lathe machines, welding equipment, and chain pulleys. Price points range from ₹12,500 to ₹3,20,000.

## Title tag rules and templates

Every product page must have a unique title tag between **50-60 characters** to avoid truncation in search results. Place the primary keyword within the first 4-5 words.

### Title tag formulas for product pages

**Standard product formula:**
```
[Product Name] [Capacity/Model] - [Key Benefit] | Hutaib Machinery
```

**Example implementations:**
- `CNC Steel Bar Bender Machine 42mm - Precision Rebar Forming | Hutaib Machinery`
- `500L Concrete Mixer - Heavy-Duty Construction Equipment | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Plate Compactor 5.5HP - Soil Compaction for Road Construction | Hutaib Machinery`

**Location-targeted product formula:**
```
[Product Name] in [City/Region] - [Specification] | Hutaib Machinery
```

**Example implementations:**
- `Rebar Bending Machine in Bengaluru - B42 & B50 Models | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Industrial Concrete Vibrator Chennai - 2HP Electric | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Road Roller Hyderabad - 1.5 Ton Compaction Equipment | Hutaib Machinery`

### Title tag formulas for product listing/category pages

**Standard category formula:**
```
[Category Name] - [Key Benefit or Range] | Hutaib Machinery
```

**Example implementations:**
- `Rebar Processing Machines - Bar Benders, Cutters & More | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Concrete Equipment - Mixers, Vibrators & Power Trowels | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Compaction Machinery - Road Rollers & Plate Compactors | Hutaib Machinery`

**Location-targeted category formula:**
```
[Category Name] in [City/State] - [Benefit/USP] | Hutaib Machinery
```

**Example implementations:**
- `Rebar Machines in Bengaluru - Full Range Available | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Concrete Equipment in Chennai - Buy from Trusted Dealer | Hutaib Machinery`
- `Construction Machinery in Hyderabad - 25+ Years Trusted | Hutaib Machinery`

### Title tag rules

- Never duplicate title tags across pages
- Include model numbers when customers search by specification
- Use numerals for measurements (42mm, 500L, 5.5HP)
- Avoid special characters except hyphens and pipes
- Correct all spelling (note: fix existing "Bangaluru" to "Bengaluru", "Bendar" to "Bender")
- Brand name "Hutaib Machinery" always at the end

## URL structure rules

URLs must follow a consistent, keyword-rich, lowercase format with hyphens.

### URL format

```
https://hutaibmachinery.com/products/[category]/[product-name-with-key-spec]
```

### URL examples

```
✅ https://hutaibmachinery.com/products/rebar-equipment/cnc-steel-bar-bender-42mm
✅ https://hutaibmachinery.com/products/concrete-machinery/concrete-mixer-500l
✅ https://hutaibmachinery.com/products/compaction-equipment/plate-compactor-5hp
✅ https://hutaibmachinery.com/locations/karnataka/bangalore

❌ https://hutaibmachinery.com/product.php?id=123
❌ https://hutaibmachinery.com/Products/CNC_Bar_Bender
❌ https://hutaibmachinery.com/p/cnc-bb
```

### URL rules

- Maximum 75 characters
- Always lowercase
- Use hyphens between words, never underscores
- Include primary product keyword
- Include key specification when relevant (42mm, 500l, 5hp)
- Remove stop words (the, and, of, for) unless necessary for clarity
- Maintain consistent trailing slash convention (with or without, but not mixed)

## Image SEO rules

All product images must be optimized for both search visibility and page speed.

### File naming conventions

**Format:** `[product-type]-[model-variant]-[descriptive-detail]-[view].webp`

**Examples:**
```
✅ cnc-bar-bender-b42-front-view.webp
✅ concrete-mixer-500l-hopper-detail.webp
✅ plate-compactor-5hp-operation-site.webp

❌ IMG_20241201_143022.jpg
❌ photo1.jpg
❌ machine_final_v2.JPG
❌ bender.png
```

### Alt text formula

**Format:** `[Brand] [Product Name] [Model/Spec] [Action/Context] [Location if relevant]`

**Examples:**
```
alt="Hutaib Machinery CNC bar bender B42 bending TMT steel rebar at construction site"
alt="500-liter concrete mixer drum loading aggregates for commercial building project"
alt="Plate compactor 5.5HP compacting soil foundation in Bengaluru construction site"
alt="Ride-on power trowel finishing concrete floor surface in warehouse"
```

### Image specifications

| Requirement | Target |
|-------------|--------|
| Format | WebP (with JPEG fallback) |
| Product images | Under 70KB each |
| Hero/banner images | Under 200KB |
| Thumbnails | Under 20KB |
| Dimensions | Actual display size (no browser scaling) |
| Multiple images per product | 4-8 images (front, side, detail, in-use) |

### Image rules

- Every product requires minimum 3 images: front view, operation view, detail view
- Always include width and height attributes in HTML
- Implement lazy loading for below-fold images
- Use srcset for responsive images
- Name files in lowercase with hyphens only
- Include the primary keyword in at least one image alt text per page
